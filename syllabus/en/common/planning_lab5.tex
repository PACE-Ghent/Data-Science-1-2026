\textbf{Goal:} choose a chart type for a given question, make the
standard \code{plotly.express} charts, add extra dimensions with colour,
facets and size, and build in simple interactivity.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Principles} -- The right chart for the question (distribution, comparison, relationship, evolution); critique a misleading chart.
  \item \textbf{\code{plotly.express} basics} -- Histogram, box plot, scatter plot and line chart, with titles, axis labels and hover information.
  \item \textbf{More dimensions} -- \code{color}, \code{facet\_col} and \code{size}; one fixed colour per position.
  \item \textbf{Heart rate as a time series} -- Zone bands with \code{add\_hrect}, and why we don't use a second y-axis.
  \item \textbf{Interactivity} -- Dropdowns and sliders with \code{mo.ui.dropdown} and \code{mo.ui.range\_slider}; optionally selections in a chart with \code{mo.ui.plotly}.
  \item \textbf{Mini-dashboard} -- 3 charts and 1 selector that at least one chart responds to.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Exercises:} (1) a histogram of weight; (2) Yo-Yo level per
position, with facets per gender; (3) speed over time for one athlete;
\textbf{assignment:} the mini-dashboard.
